## On the fix sentences

Most say what to change in CSS terms ("Set font-variant-numeric: tabular-nums", "Set text-wrap: balance", "max-width of about 65ch"). A person who builds pages by hand can use them. A person who is not an expert cannot, and no text explains the terms. Three fix sentences would do harm if followed on the pages above: widening a hidden skip link (USR-22), reshaping a round portrait (USR-29), and 65ch, which still gives lines of about 90 letters by the page's own count (USR-10).
